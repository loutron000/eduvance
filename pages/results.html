UNDERSTANDING YOURSELF

Your strongest dimensions

These dimensions show where your answers expressed the strongest interests. They are not grades or intelligence scores.

Each dimension is scored out of 30. A lower score doesn't mean you cannot develop that interest or skill.

How your results work +

Your interest profile

Each answer contributes points to one of ten interest dimensions. Your scores help describe the interests you expressed through your answers.

Career alignment

Each career has a set of dimension weights. We compare your scores with those weights to calculate a weighted interest alignment score, then rank careers across the full career list.

What the scores mean

Alignment scores are a way to organise possible pathways, not percentages of your chance of succeeding. They do not measure your intelligence, guarantee career success, or determine whether you qualify for admission.

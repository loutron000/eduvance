How Eduvance Works

A simple way to explore careers based on more than just guesswork.

Tell Us About Yourself

Eduvance starts by learning more about you. You'll answer questions about your interests, strengths, preferences, motivations, problem-solving style, and relevant academic information.

The questions are designed to reveal different aspects of who you are rather than simply asking which career you want.

We Look For Patterns

Eduvance looks at your responses across multiple dimensions rather than relying on a single answer.

These patterns help build a broader picture of the activities, environments, problems, and ways of working that may suit you.

Discover Career Matches

Based on your profile, Eduvance presents careers that may be worth exploring.

You won't get one definitive answer. Instead, you'll see a range of relevant possibilities, including careers you may already know and others you may not have considered before.

Understand The Careers

A recommendation is only useful if you understand the career behind it.

Eduvance helps you explore what different careers involve, the skills they may require, possible work environments, useful academic backgrounds, and related paths.

Explore Your Options

Use your recommendations as a starting point. Compare careers, learn more about the paths that interest you, and decide what you want to investigate further.

Eduvance doesn't make the decision for you. It helps you make a better-informed one.

Your career journey doesn't end with a recommendation.

Use what you discover as a starting point to learn more, ask questions, and explore where each path could take you.

Privacy Policy

Understand what Eduvance stores, how your information is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: October 2026

Our approach to privacy

Eduvance is a career guidance project designed to help Nigerian Senior Secondary School Three (SS3) students explore career paths and better understand their interests and strengths.

We aim to be clear about how information is handled. This policy describes the current demonstration version of Eduvance and its use of browser storage.

Information we store

Depending on how you use Eduvance, information stored in your browser may include:

  • Account details: information you provide when creating an account, such as your name, email address, and sign-in details.
  • Assessment responses: the answers you select during a career assessment, including answers saved while an assessment is unfinished.
  • Assessment results: the interest scores and career suggestions generated from a completed assessment.
  • Progress information: details needed to help the interface display your assessment or career exploration progress.

The exact information available depends on the features you use and the current implementation of the project.

How your information is used

Your information helps Eduvance to:

  • Support the demonstration sign-up and sign-in experience.
  • Save unfinished assessment progress.
  • Generate and display career guidance from your answers.
  • Show relevant results and learning roadmaps as you explore careers.

Career suggestions are intended to support exploration and reflection. They are not a final judgment of your ability, academic potential, or future success.

Browser storage and your choices

The current version of Eduvance uses your browser's localStorage to keep certain account and assessment information available between visits.

This means the information may remain on the device and browser you used until it is removed. Other people who can access that browser profile or device may also be able to access information stored there.

You can clear your browser's site data or local storage through its settings. However, doing so may remove saved account details, assessment answers, results, and progress. You may then need to start again.

Signing out should not be treated as a guarantee that all information has been erased from the browser.

Sharing and external websites

Eduvance may link to external websites, including educational platforms and career-related resources. When you follow one of these links, you leave Eduvance and become subject to the external website's own privacy practices and terms.

The current demonstration version is primarily browser-based. This policy does not promise that information is transmitted to, stored on, or deleted from a central Eduvance server. Any future version that introduces additional services or data sharing should explain those changes clearly.

Keeping your information safe

We encourage you to use Eduvance responsibly, keep access to your device private, and avoid entering sensitive personal information into a demonstration project.

Browser storage is not a substitute for a secure, production account system. The current project should not be relied on to protect confidential information, and we cannot promise that information stored on your device is completely secure.

Students and responsible use

Eduvance is intended to support students as they consider their interests, subjects, and possible careers. Students should discuss important education and career decisions with trusted parents or guardians, teachers, and qualified guidance professionals where appropriate.

Please avoid sharing passwords or other sensitive personal details with anyone. If you use a shared device, sign out when you finish and take care when saving personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Eduvance develops and its features change. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised update date.

Questions about privacy

If you have questions or concerns about this policy, please contact the Eduvance project team through its official project or repository channels.
